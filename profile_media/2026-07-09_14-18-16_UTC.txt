From June 29th to July 2nd, team Kaflah successfully held its first summer internship. The entire program was structured to merge academic development with practical and engaging experiences for the students.

The academic segment delivered interactive sessions in essential subjects, including Mathematics, English, Science, and Public Speaking. To ensure a balanced approach to student development, each day also featured specialized extracurricular modules. These activities ranged from creative arts and crafts to physical training sessions in aerobics and essential self defense. 

The internship concluded with an absolute celebration. A competitive Sports Day was hosted alongside a beautiful Art Gallery showcasing the students’ own brilliant creations over the week. To top it all off, the children received special goody bags to commemorate their hard work.

We are incredibly proud of every volunteer who contributed to making this journey a true reflection of learning, growth, and collective effort.
