On Wednesday, 28th January 2026, Team Kaflah, in collaboration with Project Zavia, organized a book reading and drawing activity at the Alif Laila Library. The session was designed to nurture creativity and instill a love for reading among children. In addition to the interactive activities, books were donated to the library, and packaged books were distributed to the children at the conclusion of the workshop, ensuring they could continue their reading journey beyond the session.
